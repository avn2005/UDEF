## Objective

The objective of this report is to evaluate three OCR technologies—Tesseract OCR, EasyOCR, and PaddleOCR—for integration into the Universal Document Extraction Framework (UDEF).

The evaluation compares installation complexity, OCR accuracy, processing speed, CPU/GPU support, licensing, and community support to recommend the most suitable OCR engine.

## Recommendation

PaddleOCR is recommended as the primary OCR engine due to its superior accuracy on structured documents and complex layouts.

EasyOCR is recommended as a secondary option for multilingual documents.

Tesseract remains valuable as a lightweight fallback because of its stability and low resource requirements.

## Conclusion

Each OCR engine has strengths and weaknesses.

Tesseract is lightweight and reliable.

EasyOCR provides good multilingual support.

PaddleOCR offers the highest overall accuracy and flexibility, making it the preferred OCR engine for the UDEF framework.
